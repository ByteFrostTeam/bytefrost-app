Questions

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing is taken away. Your progress only ever moves forward. Every pal you earned stays on your shelf, and your streak simply starts again the next day you earn a pal.

Do I have to finish everything to earn a pal?

No. Your pal is based on how much you did, not whether you did everything. 30 trophies earns bronze, 60 silver and 90 gold.

Is Improve free?

Yes. Improve is free to download and use, with up to 5 habits and 5 to-dos. Improve Premium unlocks unlimited habits and to-dos, insights, population comparisons and every theme. You can try Premium free for 3 days.

Is it available on Android?

Improve is available for iPhone today.

How do I delete my account and data?

You can delete your account from Settings in the app at any time. You can also request deletion here.
